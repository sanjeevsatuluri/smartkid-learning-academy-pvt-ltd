WHO WE ARE

Learning Today.
Leading Tomorrow.

SmartKid Learning Academy Private Limited is an education and skill-development company focused on creating engaging learning experiences for children and meaningful opportunities for schools and educational institutions.

SmartKid's journey is rooted in programmes such as Abacus, Vedic Mathematics, English, Phonics, Handwriting, Drawing & Painting, Chess, Rubik's Cube, AI & Coding, Robotics, Soft Skills and Spoken English.

Today, that foundation is expanding into a broader educational ecosystem covering academic programmes, skill development, technology, assessments, competitions and SMART Olympiad.

THE ORGANIZATION

SmartKid Learning Academy Private Limited

SmartKid Learning Academy Private Limited is incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 and operates in the field of education and skill development. Its registered objects cover educational programmes, training, assessments, Olympiads and competitions, educational technology, publications and related learning activities.

Corporate registration establishes the company and its stated business objects; it should not be interpreted as Government endorsement or accreditation of individual programmes.
